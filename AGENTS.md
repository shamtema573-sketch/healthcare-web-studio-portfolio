# Project instructions

- Use skills/healthcare-web-studio/SKILL.md and its anti-template checklist when changing healthcare-facing pages.
- Keep the four demo identities visually distinct and do not invent medical credentials, testimonials, outcomes, or clinical guarantees.
- Mark clinic names, practitioners, prices, locations, and interaction states as fictional demo content.
- Do not add real patient data, payments, or external submission without a separately approved integration design.
- Use local, licensed, or generated assets; record provenance in docs/09_ASSETS_AND_LICENSES.md.
- Before publishing changes, run the documented typecheck, build, and browser QA commands.
