## Design decisions

- Each project keeps a different content structure and visual direction. Booking actions are also distinct: a photo-led service/time panel (Aurelia), a visit/practitioner board (Forma), a sample-collection route ticket (Clarity), and a session-format planner (MoveWell).
- Navigation, catalog filters, and add controls received larger type or touch targets. The main UX issues from the supplied screenshots were addressed without changing the overall visual family unnecessarily.
- New imagery uses fictional adults and practitioners in natural face-to-face scenes. Aurelia and Forma no longer show a tablet in their consultations; Clarity uses a fictional laboratory scientist. European/Central European appearance is a casting direction for the concepts, not a claim about an actual person's identity or qualifications.
- Clinic names, service prices, team profiles, locations, and booking states remain demo content. Forms do not transmit or store personal or medical data.
